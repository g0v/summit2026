Support g0v Summit

g0v Summit is a volunteer-organized, non-profit event. “Invite Yourself” is our theme for 2026 — we invite everyone to pull up a stool and become part of the action. The sponsors listed above are the key supporters who make g0v Summit 2026 possible.

Every g0v Summit depends on the support and participation of the community. Beyond attending sessions and events, you’re welcome to support the open community through sponsorship.

We offer a variety of partnership packages including brand exposure, booth setup, ticket sponsorship, and more custom options. We look forward to working with you to advance civic technology.

For sponsorship and partnership inquiries, please contact: sponsorship@summit.g0v.tw
