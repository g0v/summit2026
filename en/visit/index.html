Humanities & Social Sciences Building, Academia Sinica

128 Academia Rd. Sec. 2, Nangang District, Taipei, Taiwan
OpenStreetMap Google Maps

Transportation

Reaching Nangang

  • Ride Taiwan Railway or Taiwan High Speed Rail to Nangang station.
  • Ride Taipei Metro to Nangang station (Bannan Line) or Nangang Exhibition Center station (Bannan Line & Wenhu Line).

Reaching Academia Sinica

  • From MRT Nangang station Exit 2, take route 212 Express, 270, or Blue 25 to Academia Sinica.
  • From MRT Nangang Exhibition Center station Exit 5, take route 205, 306, 620, 645, 679, S1, S5 or S12 to Academia Sinica.
  • Walk or ride YouBike to Humanities and Social Science Building.

20-minute interval. Please keep an eye out for conference staff holding shuttle bus sign outside of the station and arrive early for an effortless ride to the venue.

1. To the Venue

Summit Shuttle Bus operates daily from 7:45 to 10:10 between TRA Nangang StationNorth Entrance, HSSB, Academia Sinica.

2. To the Station

Summit Shuttle Bus operates daily from 17:00 to 18:30 between North Entrance, HSSB, Academia SinicaTRA Nangang Station.

Driving Route

  • From National Highway No. 3, exit at Nanshen Rd., turn left onto Jiuzhuang St. Sec. 1, and then reach Academia Sinica on Academia Rd. Sec. 2.

Vehicle Access

  • Drivers shall exchange a temporary parking permit at the entrance and park responsibly according to campus rules.

Motorcycle Parking

  • As motorbikes are prohibited from entering the campus, please utilize free parking spaces next to Academia Sinica’s main entrance and side entrances.
